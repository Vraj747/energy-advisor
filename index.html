Estimate your electricity cost

Enter how much electricity you use in a month and what you pay per kilowatt-hour (kWh). Both numbers are on your electricity bill.

kWh

In kilowatt-hours, for one month. Example: 750.

¢ / kWh

In cents per kWh. Example: 12.0 means 12 cents.

How this works

  1. You enter your monthly use and rate.
  2. We multiply them to estimate your monthly, yearly and daily cost.
  3. You test a savings idea, such as using 10% less.

Nothing you type leaves your device.
